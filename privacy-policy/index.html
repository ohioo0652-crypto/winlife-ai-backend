SoluluMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

SoluluMind ("SoluluMind", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SoluluMind handles information when you use the SoluluMind application.

1. Information We Handle

SoluluMind is designed so that most information you create in the app is stored locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Your chosen name or nickname
  • Goals, dreams, and intentions
  • Journal entries and reflections
  • Mood and sleep information that you choose to record
  • Routines and routine items
  • Calendar tasks and to-do items
  • Wins, achievements, XP, levels, and streaks
  • Affirmations and favorites
  • Prayer selections and related activity
  • Vision-board content, including images and quotes
  • Alter Ego information and entries
  • Focus-timer activity
  • Custom frequencies and other custom content
  • App settings, themes, language preferences, and other preferences
  • AI conversation history stored within the app

Most of this information remains on your device.

2. AI Features

SoluluMind includes an optional AI companion called Solulu.

When you choose to use an AI feature, information needed to process your request is sent from the app to our backend and then processed using Google Gemini.

Depending on your use of the feature, this may include:

  • Your AI message
  • Your chosen name
  • Your goal or dream
  • Your latest mood information
  • Your wins
  • Your streak or activity information
  • Recent AI conversation history

We do not intentionally send your entire locally stored SoluluMind data to Gemini. Only information included in an AI request is transmitted.

3. Google Gemini

SoluluMind uses Google Gemini through our backend to generate AI responses. Information sent to Gemini may be processed according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Users should avoid entering highly sensitive personal information into AI conversations.

4. Local Storage

Much of SoluluMind's information is stored locally on your device. This includes journal entries, mood information, calendar tasks, goals, routines, streaks, vision-board content, affirmations, reflections, and app preferences.

5. Vision Board

SoluluMind allows you to add images and quotes to a personal vision board. Vision-board content is stored as part of your locally stored app data. Adding an image to your vision board does not by itself send that image to our AI backend.

6. Advertising

SoluluMind does not intentionally use advertising SDKs or sell personal information for advertising purposes.

7. Data Security

AI requests are transmitted using HTTPS when communicating with the production backend.

We use reasonable measures to protect information processed through the service. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, reset the app's stored data, clear the application's storage, or uninstall the application.

Information transmitted through the AI feature may be processed by our backend and Google Gemini according to their applicable service terms and retention policies.

9. Your Choices

You can choose whether to use the optional AI features.

You can manage locally stored information using the data-management controls provided by the app.

Avoid entering passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information into AI conversations.

10. Children's Privacy

SoluluMind is a general lifestyle and personal-growth application and is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts or profiles.

11. Third-Party Services

SoluluMind uses third-party services necessary to provide certain functionality, including Google Gemini for AI processing.

Those services may process information transmitted to them as necessary to provide their services.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features or data practices change.

The "Last updated" date will be updated when material changes are made.
